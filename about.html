About Life on Land

We're dedicated to raising awareness about SDG 15 and mobilizing action to protect terrestrial ecosystems, combat biodiversity loss, and promote sustainable land use globally.

🎯 Our Mission

To educate, inform, and inspire action on SDG 15 by providing accessible, evidence-based information about terrestrial ecosystem protection, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable land management.

  • Make SDG 15 data and targets accessible to everyone
  • Connect people with actionable conservation opportunities
  • Highlight the global biodiversity crisis
  • Support informed decision-making on environmental policy

🌍 Our Vision

A world where terrestrial ecosystems are protected and restored, biodiversity thrives, and land is managed sustainably to support both human wellbeing and nature.

  • Zero net loss of forests by 2030
  • Protected and restored habitats for all species
  • Sustainable agriculture and land use practices globally
  • Indigenous communities empowered as conservation leaders

Our Approach

We combine evidence-based information with actionable pathways to drive real-world change.

1

Evidence-Based Information

All data and targets come from authoritative sources: UN SDGs, IPCC, IUCN, Global Forest Watch, and peer-reviewed research. We present the facts clearly and honestly.

2

Systems Thinking

We recognize that ecosystem protection, climate action, sustainable development, and social justice are interconnected. Solutions must address all dimensions.

3

Multi-Level Action

Change happens at individual, community, national, and global levels. We provide pathways for everyone to contribute — from lifestyle changes to policy advocacy.

4

Inclusive & Accessible

We believe conservation knowledge should be free and accessible to all. Our content is available in multiple formats and languages, with a focus on clarity over jargon.

5

Community-Centered

Indigenous peoples and local communities are nature's best stewards. We highlight their leadership, respect their rights, and amplify their voices in conservation.

6

Solutions-Oriented

We focus on what works. Featuring proven conservation strategies, restoration technologies, policy successes, and real examples of positive change.
