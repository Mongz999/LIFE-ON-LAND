🌱 Individual Actions

Small daily choices compound into significant impact. Here are concrete steps you can take:

Reduce consumption

Why: Overproduction drives habitat destruction, deforestation, and pollution.

  • Buy less, choose quality over quantity
  • Repair and reuse instead of discarding
  • Support circular economy brands
  • Reduce plastic use (bags, packaging, single-use items)

Dietary choices

Why: Livestock farming is a leading cause of deforestation, especially in the Amazon.

  • Reduce meat consumption (especially beef)
  • Choose sustainably-caught or plant-based proteins
  • Buy local, seasonal produce
  • Support regenerative agriculture

Support sustainable products

Why: Certified products fund conservation and enforce ethical practices.

  • Look for FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certification on wood products
  • Choose MSC (Marine Stewardship Council) certified seafood
  • Buy organic and fair-trade certified goods
  • Support indigenous-led conservation initiatives

Energy & transportation

Why: Climate change is a major driver of ecosystem collapse.

  • Switch to renewable energy if possible
  • Reduce transportation emissions: walk, bike, public transit, or carpool
  • Offset carbon through verified projects
  • Reduce energy use at home (heating, cooling, appliances)

Support wildlife protection

Why: Illegal wildlife trade is a $23B industry threatening species with extinction.

  • Never purchase wildlife products (ivory, skins, exotic pets)
  • Report poaching/trafficking to Wildlife Justice Commission
  • Support anti-poaching organizations
  • Educate others about the wildlife trade crisis

Home & garden biodiversity

Why: Urban and suburban habitats are critical for insects, birds, and pollinators.

  • Plant native species in your garden
  • Avoid pesticides and herbicides
  • Create water sources (bird baths, small ponds)
  • Leave dead wood for insects and fungi

Donation Tips

  • Research the organization: Check ratings on Charity Navigator or GuideStar
  • Specify your cause: Many organizations allow you to designate donations to specific projects (e.g., Amazon protection, marine conservation)
  • Set up recurring donations: Monthly donations provide organizations with predictable funding
  • Tax deductions: In most countries, donations to registered nonprofits are tax-deductible — consult your local tax authority
  • Corporate matching: Many employers match charitable donations — check your HR department

📢 Policy & Advocacy

Use your voice to influence policy and hold governments & corporations accountable:

Contact elected representatives

Legislators respond to constituent pressure. Contact your government officials on:

  • Climate change legislation and net-zero commitments
  • Protected area designation and funding
  • Wildlife trafficking and poaching enforcement
  • Sustainable agriculture and forestry policies
  • Indigenous land rights and recognition

How: Find your representatives at House.gov or equivalent in your country

Support campaigns

Join organized campaigns targeting specific issues:

  • Campaign for Nature — 30% protected areas by 2030
  • Global Forest Watch campaigns — Zero deforestation
  • WWF campaigns — Climate, wildlife, oceans
  • The Petition Site — environmental campaigns

Corporate accountability

Pressure companies on environmental practices:

  • Demand supply chain transparency (deforestation-free, ethical sourcing)
  • Support sustainable brands and boycott unsustainable ones
  • File shareholder resolutions on climate and biodiversity
  • Leave reviews and feedback on company environmental commitments

Resources: Rainforest Foundation, Interfaith Center on Corporate Responsibility

Raise awareness & educate

Spread knowledge to amplify impact:

  • Share facts and resources on social media
  • Host screenings of environmental documentaries
  • Organize community talks and workshops
  • Write letters to newspaper editors
  • Start a blog or podcast on biodiversity
