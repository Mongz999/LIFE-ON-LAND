Did You Know?

🌲 Forest Facts

Forests cover 31% of global land, but we lose about 10 million hectares per year.

🦁 Biodiversity

One million species face extinction — that's 1 every 5 minutes due to habitat loss.

🌍 Climate Impact

Forests absorb 2.4 billion tons of CO₂ annually, making them crucial for climate stability.

👥 Human Connection

1.6 billion people depend on forests for their livelihoods and survival.
