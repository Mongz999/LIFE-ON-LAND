Protect, restore and promote sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems, sustainably manage forests, combat desertification, and halt and reverse land degradation and halt biodiversity loss.

The natural world is in crisis. Half of all forest cover has been lost. One million species face extinction. Urgent action to protect and restore ecosystems is essential.

Why It Matters

🌍 Biodiversity crisis

One million animal and plant species are threatened with extinction. Habitat destruction, pollution and climate change are the primary drivers.

🌳 Forest loss

Forests cover 31% of global land. They absorb CO₂, support 80% of terrestrial species, and provide livelihoods for 1.6 billion people.

⚠️ Desertification

Land degradation affects 1.3 billion people. Dryland ecosystems are shrinking, reducing crop yields and exacerbating poverty.

💚 Carbon storage

Terrestrial ecosystems store twice as much carbon as the atmosphere. Protecting them is critical for climate action.

Progress on SDG 15

50% Forest lost globally

Half of the world's forests have disappeared since the beginning of civilization.

1M+ Species at risk

Species extinction rates are 1,000 times higher than the natural baseline.

17% Land protected

Protected areas now cover 17% of terrestrial and inland water ecosystems.

$$ Gap Funding shortfall

The annual biodiversity finance gap exceeds $700 billion.
